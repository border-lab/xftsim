● Key takeaways:

  - Meiosis is fast — 105ms for 10k individuals x 5k variants (numba JIT)
  - Matvec is BLAS-bound — 89ms raw, 200ms standardized at 10k x 5k
  - Full simulation at realistic scale (5k x 2k, 5 gens) is under 0.5s
  - I/O checkpoint is the bottleneck — 4.1s at 5k x 2k due to
  np.savez_compressed
  - Founder generation is the other slow spot — ~1s at 10k x 5k (Python-level
  binomial loop)

  The simulation loop itself is very efficient; I/O and founder generation are
  the places where optimization effort would pay off most if you ever need to
  scale up.
