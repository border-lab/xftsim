- Retention policy prunes gen 0 — tests need `retain_*=10` or iterate over available keys
- Component kind names: 'generative' (not 'noise'), 'aggregating' (not 'aggregation'), 'reference' (not 'parental')
- CNoiseComponent takes `cov=` (not `cov_matrix=`)
