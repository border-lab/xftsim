  The foundation of the refactored xftsim rests on three core abstractions:

  - iid: Individual IDs (array of strings)

  - chrom: Chromosome numbers
  - pos_bp: Base pair positions
  - pos_cM: Centimorgan positions (for recombination)
  - af: Allele frequencies
  - ref/alt: Reference and alternate alleles
  - Supports subsetting and conversion between haploid/diploid indexing

  - samples: SampleMeta and variants: VariantMeta as attributes
  - matvec(effects) -> phenotypes: Compute G @ beta

  - __getitem__: Subsetting by samples or variants, returns same type
  - shape: (n_samples, n_haploid_variants) - note haploid representation

  - DenseHaplotypeArray: NumPy-backed, stores (n, m, 2) array internally
  - GraphHaplotypeOperator: Wraps grapp/glink (Phase 4)
